# Refresh account

One account kept current: what the owner's calls, email and people say, turned into suggestions they approve in CodyNote › Claude suggestions. Six modes, each in its own file next to this one: read only the files of the modes you run.

| Mode | The owner says | Read | Ends with |
|---|---|---|---|
| **call** | "I just finished with Acme", "wrap up my Acme call", "after the call with Acme", "process my call with Acme", "update the read on Acme after my call"; "with email" adds the call participants' email | `mode-call.md` | a scan, the follow-up email draft and HubSpot note, the items tidy |
| **email** | "catch up on email for Acme", "sync Acme's email", "read Acme's email since last time", "what's in my email with Acme" | `mode-email.md` | a scan |
| **people** | "profile the people at Acme", "who's who at Acme", "what do I know about Dana", "update Dana's profile", "profile Sam" (a teammate) | `mode-people.md` | a scan (people only) |
| **items** | "tidy Acme's items", "clean up Acme's items", "clean up duplicate items on Acme", "any doubled-up items on Acme?", "tidy items on Acme and Globex" | `mode-items.md` | the "bring items up to the rules" pass (0.53.0), also the last step of every other mode: for every open item a Situation, a "Topic: …" title, a three-part Situation, a next step naming a day, its storyline, folds for lookalikes; the read's Do first and Situation; in rounds the owner approves |
| **deals** | "refresh Acme's deals", "update Acme's deals from HubSpot", "are Acme's deals current?"; "import Acme from HubSpot", "bring Acme in from HubSpot" (adds the company first when it's new) | `mode-deals.md` | deals and lines by the one deal rule (`codynote_hubspot_deals`); a new company, its Managed by and product statuses |
| **verify** | "check Acme", "what's done on Acme", "is the Dallas quote still open?", "check Acme's items"; also the last step of every "with email" mode | `mode-verify.md` | one check per open thing (`codynote_get_verify_brief`): done with proof, still open, no longer true or unclear |
| **refresh** | "refresh Acme", "refresh Acme with email" | step 3 below | one scan for the modes with new material |

"Refresh **me** on Acme" asks for a briefing (the Account brief skill, read-only), not this skill; if it isn't clear which the owner wants, ask.

Every mode: everything goes in **one pass**: a new contact's profile lines, attendee and speaker names and emails, a new deal's lines, a new item's lines go in the same call as the add, by `ref`, or by its waiting suggestion's id from an earlier call (`account-picture.md`, "One pass"); never stop to ask the owner to approve some first and say go. **Never a "fix by hand" list:** what's wrong in CodyNote gets its fix suggested (`account-picture.md`, "Corrections": supersede your own wrong line, re-date a box with `as_of`, change a meeting's date (its lines follow on approval), merge Rings or set a Ring's `role` / `new_ring`, `replaced_by`, a `looks_won` signal, a follow-up cleared, an item done with `done_proof`); what's wrong in HubSpot goes in the call's `hubspot_fix`, not prose; what the owner typed stays theirs (say it in the reply). Nothing is saved except **suggestions** through `codynote_suggest_changes`; never send anything; HubSpot is read-only; Outlook email is read for the account's update **only** in email mode, verify mode (a check names the account), the item checks (0.54.0, below) or when the owner adds "with email". The shared files are next to this one: `work-order.md` (the order of an update), `cards.md` (the boxes and the person rules), `account-picture.md` (the shapes), `panzura.md` (the Panzura angle) and, only when reading email, `email.md`.

**For field X, follow its recipe** (`field-guide.md` next to this file, one line per field; `codynote_recipe(id)` has the whole recipe: where to look in order, what counts, the format, what never to do). Never stop for an approval: the owner approves each suggestion in CodyNote.

**Items read cold (0.52.0, rules 0.53.0).** Every mode that touches an action item writes its **Situation** (recipe `item.situation`) in three labeled parts, each its own short paragraph: "The ask:" (what was asked or promised, by whom, with their role), "Where it stands:" (what has happened, who has the next move), "Why it matters:" (the renewal, deadline or storyline it bears on); never its Goal (retired: the connector refuses `goal`; the Goals already there stay). The title is "Topic: what exactly, how much, for whom" (recipe `item.text`, at most 90, no leading "do this" verb), one item per topic: fold first (recipe `item.pick`, a topic already open gets an update, not a new item), a follow-up "waiting on <name, role> since <date>" from any source (meeting, email, ticket). Read an item's Situation first; its Goal only on an old item that has no Situation yet (`goal_before_situation` once it has one). A new item carries `situation`, `done_when` and `next_step` (who / what / when, naming a day or "this week"); on an existing one the Situation is rewritten whole (a `box` change with `as_of`) when the story changed.

**The cleanup (0.53.0).** Before the reply, every mode ends the same way: bring the account's open items that do not meet the rules up to them (title "Topic: what exactly, how much, for whom", a three-part Situation, a next step who / what / when, folds, follow-ups), as `mode-items.md` describes, in rounds of at most 12 cards. There is no separate cleanup skill: the owner's way is account by account, inside the normal process. An item that already passes gets nothing.

**Item checks read every source (0.54.0).** Every mode that updates action items also checks every open item on the account: verify mode's steps 1 to 3 with `scope: "items"` (`mode-verify.md`), in the brief's `plan.order`: CodyNote → Fathom (calls not in CodyNote yet, `codynote_fathom_missing(company, since)`) → Outlook → Teams → everything new in HubSpot, each matched against every open item. Outlook and Teams are read every time, "with email" or not: a check names the account. Teams and HubSpot may not be connected on this computer: **try each, and when a source can't be read (no tool, no permission, an error), skip it and say so** (`searched` gets `{source, looked_for: "skipped: <why>"}`, the reply one line), then carry on with the rest. Still-open and unclear checks are saved quietly (the answer's `checks_recorded`); only a change (done, no longer true) waits for the owner. An item whose **Summary** (recipe `item.summary`) is missing or no longer matches it gets one in the same pass.

## 1. Check CodyNote, once

`codynote_ping(skills: "0.55.0")` before anything else (the `skills` value tells CodyNote which skills Claude has). Its `version` must be **0.55.0** or later (an older CodyNote answers in one line, without `version` and `tools`): if it is older, say "Update CodyNote to 0.55.0 or later, then ask again" and stop. Call only the tools in its `tools`. No mode carries a fallback for an older CodyNote.

## 2. Pick the mode, then follow its file

The owner's words pick the mode (the table). A person named alone ("what do I know about Dana") is people mode for that person. Several modes asked at once ("wrap up my Acme call and tidy its items"): work them in the order call, email, people, items, in one scan where they share one (step 3), and verify last. **Every "with email" mode ends with verify mode** (`mode-verify.md`, the whole account): the email is already read, so most checks cost nothing more.

## 3. Refresh: the modes with new material, in one scan

"Refresh <Company>" (or "refresh <Company> with email") updates the account from whatever is new, as one update:

1. **Open it once:** call mode's steps 1.1 and 1.3 (`codynote_get_customer` with `detail: "full"`, then `codynote_get_update_brief`; keep its `scan_id` for every suggest call, `work_order`, and the brief's `email` block).
2. **Which modes have new material:**
   - **call** when the brief lists new material (`new_material`: meetings, transcripts, documents, links). Read it as call mode's step 2, over all of it, not only the newest call.
   - **email** only with "with email": email mode's step 2 (the whole account since `emails_through`) in place of call mode's 30-day participants search. No `outlook_email_search` tool, or no domains and no addresses in the brief's `email` block: say so in one line, set coverage email `"skipped: <why>"` and carry on with the rest. Without "with email", coverage says email `"not asked"`.
   - **people** when the brief's `people.unnamed_speakers` lists labels, or someone who spoke or wrote in the new material has no Observations yet (`people` in the briefing): for them, read as people mode's step 2 (every call and email reference on the account). Merging copies of a person (people mode's step 1.3) is not part of a refresh: say "profile the people at <Company>" for that.
   - **items** always, after the scan: call mode's step 7 (the folds), then the item checks (above). The whole cleanup pass (`mode-items.md`) only when the owner asks for it ("tidy Acme's items").
3. **Suggest once, in the work order:** one `codynote_suggest_changes` call with `scan_id`, as call mode's step 4 over everything read: the call material, and with email email mode's step 3 (the `email` records and `email_sync` first, then what the emails support, each sourced to its email). In the People step, the people from step 2 get people mode's step 3 (at most 6 observations each, the person rules in `cards.md`); everyone else gets call mode's two or three.
4. **One `close_scan`** (call mode's step 5) with honest `coverage` for the whole update. With "with email", then verify mode (`mode-verify.md`) over the account, reusing what the emails showed. Then the items tidy (call mode's step 7: `codynote_lookalikes` with `kind: "items"`, folds sent as `merge` suggestions without waiting for go, as after a call). Not call mode's step 6: when the newest new meeting is a call, end the reply with "Want the follow-up email and HubSpot note for the <date> call?"
5. **Nothing new** (the brief says `nothing_new`, no "with email", no unnamed speakers): close the scan with every step `"nothing new"` (email `"not asked"`), no read, still run the items tidy, and say "<Company> is current: nothing new since <date of the last update>."

Reply as call mode's step 8 without its **Follow-up email** and **For HubSpot** parts, with a first line naming what ran: `**Modes:** call ✓ · email not asked · people: 2 new profiles · items: 1 pair folded`.
