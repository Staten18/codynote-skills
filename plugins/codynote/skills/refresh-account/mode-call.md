# Call mode (was After the call)

Refresh account's call mode: "I just finished with Acme", "wrap up my Acme call", "after the call with Acme", "process my call with Acme", "update the read on Acme after my call", or any of these "with email". From the call in CodyNote (pasted from Fathom or OneNote), work the account in CodyNote's work order and suggest people and attendee matches, speaker names, profile observations for the people who spoke, Ring environment details, deal changes, storyline updates, action items with their boxes, blank company facts, a fresh read and folds of open items typed twice; then draft the follow-up email and a HubSpot note.

The owner just got off a call. Turn it into suggestions that keep the account current, a follow-up email draft and a HubSpot note. They approve everything in CodyNote. Be quick and keep the reply short: this runs after most calls.

Nothing is saved except **suggestions** through `codynote_suggest_changes`. Never send the email. HubSpot is read-only: write the note for the owner to paste. Email is read **only** when the owner adds "with email" (step 3).

Work in the order of `work-order.md` (next to this file): the sections below follow it. `cards.md` has every card's boxes and the `box` / `line` / `tick` / `split` shapes; `account-picture.md` the model, the lint kinds and the other shapes; `panzura.md` the Panzura angle; with "with email", `email.md`.

**For field X, follow its recipe** (`field-guide.md` next to this file, one line per field; `codynote_recipe(id)` has the whole recipe: where to look in order, what counts, the format, what never to do). Never stop for an approval: the owner approves each suggestion in CodyNote.

## 1. Find the call and open the scan

1. `codynote_get_customer` with the name and `detail: "full"`. If it matches more than one account, or none, ask which and stop. Keep `overview.company_id`, the read, sentiment, storylines, facts, `needs_a_look`, `meetings`, `open_items`, `contacts`, `people` (each person's persona, sentiment and newest Observations), `deals`, `products`, `rings` (with `ring_id`), `environment_history`; on an account managed by an MSP (one of the owner's accounts, a Panzura partner), `managed_by` and `managed_by_people` (the MSP's contacts with their profiles, read from the MSP, each marked `from`): the MSP's people are often on this account's calls.
2. The call is the newest meeting (or the one the owner names). If it isn't there and Fathom (if connected) has it, tell the owner: "Paste the Fathom summary and transcript into CodyNote (Meetings › Paste Fathom summary), then say done." Still draft the email and HubSpot note from Fathom's summary, then stop.
3. `codynote_get_update_brief` with the company, and follow it. Keep its `scan_id` (on every suggest call below), `work_order`, `cards`, the boxes of each open item and storyline (with line ids and `set_by`), what the owner rejected or removed (never suggest those again), and which facts are blank.

## 2. Read (no suggestions yet)

`codynote_read` with the call's `meeting_id` (from `codynote_map` or `meetings`): its `speakers` list each "Speaker N" label (`name: null` when unnamed) and the text reads with names where they're known. With a transcript, `find` what matters: problems, projects, tools and vendors, people and roles, dates, numbers (TB, users, sites), commitments on both sides, competitors. Read any other new material the brief lists the same way. If the brief says nothing is new, still work the steps below from the call itself: the owner may have run "update the overview" first.

## 3. With email (only when the owner said "with email")

The recipe in `email.md`, limited to the call's participants (the attendees with an address in `contacts`) and the last 30 days: for each address, `sender: <address>` and `recipient: <address>`, `afterDateTime` 30 days ago. An `email` record per kept email; what the emails support goes into the steps below, sourced to its email; the look-back if this run opens a storyline. **No `email_sync`**: this search isn't the whole account's, so the watermark stays. Without "with email", coverage says `"not asked"`.

## 4. Suggest, in the work order (one `codynote_suggest_changes` call with `scan_id`)

Check `open_items`, `contacts`, `deals`, `products` first so nothing repeats what the owner just added from Fathom. Each change has a one-line reason and the call (or its email, or a web page) as the source. Give each change a `confidence` (`high`, `medium` or `low`: how sure you are), and copy every quote exactly as it is in the source: CodyNote looks for a meeting's quote in its transcript and marks one it can't find "quote not found". Put the changes in this order:

1. **People:** new or changed contacts, a contact's `role` (persona) when the call makes it plain, each attendee matched to a contact (`attendee`, shapes in `email.md`; `people` includes those linked to this account from another, `linked: true`). Before any new contact, look them up (`codynote_read` with their `name`, every account): someone already in CodyNote on another account (a partner's account executive, say) gets a `link` (`{"action": "link", "record": "contact", "id": <their person_id>, "company": <this account>}`), never a second contact; add only when nobody matches (`account-picture.md`, "People across accounts"), with `ref` (`c1`) so their attendee, speaker name, Observations and boxes go in this same call with `"c1"` where their `person_id` goes (`account-picture.md`, "One pass"); the same for a new deal's lines and a new item's lines. On a managed account match the attendees and speakers against `managed_by_people` too: an MSP person is never added as a new contact here (they live on the MSP), and an `attendee` link takes the account's own contacts only, so for them name the speaker (`speaker` with their `contact_id` is accepted) and add their Observations under this account (`card: "person"`, `id` = their `person_id` from `managed_by_people`); a change to the MSP person's contact record goes under the MSP (`company`: the MSP's name). Someone the call says retired, left or changed role ("Lee retired last month"): a `contact` change `{record: "contact", action: "change", fields: {active: false}}` with that line as the quote, and the replacement it names as a new contact (`account-picture.md`, "Day to day"). Then, briefly, in the order of `work-order.md`: each unnamed speaker the transcript makes plain named (`speaker`, the line that shows it as the reason: "Thanks, Dana" after Speaker 2; shape in `account-picture.md`) → for each person who spoke, an **Observations** `line` (`card: "person"`, `id` = their `person_id`) per thing worth noticing under the person rules in `cards.md` (never a setup fact or what others said), dated the call's day with the quote → the boxes those support (a `sentiment` box when it moved, an `interests` or `sore_spots` line). Two or three per person at most, nothing already on their card; people mode ("profile the people at <Company>") does the deep pass. **Never** the company's sentiment (the owner's).
2. **Account and environment:** the account's **goal** and **next meeting** when the call says so (0.50.0): a `company` change with `goal` (what the customer wants from Panzura this year, one sentence; recipe `company.goal`; replace one the owner typed only when the call shows the goal changed, quoting the old one in the reason) and `next_meeting_on` (the day the call set for the next meeting, today or later; recipe `company.next_meeting_on`; with "with email" the Outlook calendar invite counts too; a past `next_meeting_on` is replaced by the next one the call names); a TB figure → an `mc_reading` on the right ring; a CloudFS version, node count, object store, hypervisor or HA detail → a `ring` change (`id` = its `ring_id`, camelCase fields, only what was said; several Rings and the call doesn't say which → the Heads-up instead); a product they asked about → `product_status` "discussed".
3. **Deals:** a new close date, stage, forecast or term, or a new opportunity. **Never** Closed Won / Lost: a deal that sounds won gets a `looks_won` `signal` (and the reply says so); a current renewal a later won one replaced gets `replaced_by` (`account-picture.md`, "Corrections").
4. **Storylines.** For each one the call touches, in this order: a History line dated the day it happened → Open questions it answered ticked (`tick`, the line id from the brief, the call as source) and new ones added (`line`) → Helps / Hurts / Signals to watch lines (research the thing itself with a web search when it's new, and match it to `panzura.md`) → Now rewritten whole → status (`resolved` with an outcome; `watch` with the date it waits on; a watched one moving again → `open`). Then new storylines: kind, one line, Now, first History line and the lines it has. A `watch_due` or `now_behind` in `needs_a_look` that the call settles gets its fix here.
5. **Action items** (0.53.0, recipe `item.pick`): read the transcript plus **both** Fathom lists (Action items and Next steps overlap: the same ask often sits in both and in the transcript) as **one pool**. The app no longer offers Fathom lines for Add or Decline: you sort them. Above all what the owner now **waits on them** for (`status: "waiting"`, from any source) and handoffs to teammates. **Fold first:** read the account's open items (`codynote_list_action_items` with the company and `mine_only: false`); a topic already open, in any wording, gets an update of that item (a Context `line` and a new `next_step`), not a new item. A matching add becomes that update anyway; a match with a waiting suggestion or another add in the same call is refused; add `new_item: true` (with the call as source) only when it really is new and CodyNote would mistake it for an open one (`account-picture.md`, "No duplicate items"). More than about five new items from one meeting means fold harder. New: `fields: {text, due_date, priority, status, meeting_id, boxes: {situation, done_when, next_step}, lines: {context: [...]}}`: the title "Topic: what exactly, how much, for whom" (recipe `item.text`, at most 90, no leading "do this" verb), the Situation in three labeled parts "The ask:", "Where it stands:", "Why it matters:" (recipe `item.situation`; never a `goal`, which the connector refuses), the next step who / what / when (recipe `item.next_step`, naming a day or "this week"), `due_date` only when the call states or implies one, a short quote from the call as the source's evidence. On an existing item the call moved: a Context `line` for what was said, a new `next_step` `box` (never over one with `set_by: "owner"`: say it in the reply instead) and, when the story changed, the `situation` `box` rewritten whole with `as_of` the call's day (read its Situation first; its Goal only on an old item without one). One the call shows done: `status: "done"` with `done_proof` (the call: `{kind: "meeting", id, title, date}`); a waiting one they answered: the `follow_up` cleared (`account-picture.md`, "Corrections").
   **A queued meeting run.** When the owner pastes a meeting into CodyNote, the app queues a run "Wrap up <meeting>" (scope kind `meeting`, its record the meeting's id). Do it as call mode for that one meeting: read it with `codynote_read`, work this step's pool, and cover the rest of the call steps only as far as that meeting says.
6. **Company facts:** only fields the brief lists as blank. Web search the company (its site, filings, a reputable profile); cite the page and the date. A `fact_old` Claude set → a fresh one. Skip what you can't source.

**Split:** each card the brief shows with Notes and no boxes gets one `split` of its Notes into the boxes, once; Notes keeps its text.

## 5. Finish the scan (`close_scan`)

One last `codynote_suggest_changes` call with `scan_id`, `summary`, and:
- **Knowledge** bullets (Tech stack, Workloads, Use cases) in `changes`, as the brief's bullet actions (`{company, section, action: "add_bullet" | "update" | "mention" | "add_details" | "outdated", ...}`), with short researched details, no padding;
- `close_scan: {company, coverage, read}`, with **the read** if anything material changed (always when `read_behind` is listed and the call says enough): reason over the whole account as it will be once approved (storylines, deals and renewal, MC vs licensed, open and waiting items, the owner's sentiment). Health with a reason; "my read" on inferences;
- and **`coverage`**, one entry per step: the count suggested, `"nothing new"`, `"skipped: <why>"`, or for email `"not asked"`. Be honest: the owner sees it.

## 6. Follow-up email and HubSpot note

**Email** (draft only, the owner's voice: warm, direct, short): thanks; a 2-4 line recap; agreed next steps as a list with who and by when; links or files promised. No internal numbers, no pricing unless discussed, nothing about competitors. With email, answer what's still open in the threads.

**HubSpot note** (2-4 lines to paste): date and type of call, what changed, next steps with dates.

## 7. Tidy the account's items (the cleanup)

First, bring the account's open items that do not meet the rules up to them (0.53.0: "Topic: …" titles, three-part Situations, next steps who / what / when, follow-ups: `mode-items.md`, steps 2 and 3). Then, last, `codynote_lookalikes` with `kind: "items"` and `company_id: overview.company_id`. For each `same` pair that really is one task (read both items and their Context lines: one thing said two ways, not two steps of a project), suggest the fold in one `codynote_suggest_changes` call without `scan_id` (the scan is finished): one `merge` on record `action_item` with `fields: {keep_id, fold_id}` and a `reason` that says why they are one task (shape in `account-picture.md`, "Tidy"; recipe `item.fold`); Claude proposes, the owner approves, one Undo puts everything back. This account only. Never fold a pair the tool marks `hold` or doesn't list, nor two items with different owners unless the call's summary says they are one task. Say what you folded and what you left.

Then the item checks (0.54.0, every call, "with email" or not): verify mode (`mode-verify.md`) with `scope: "items"` over every open item, reading every source in order (CodyNote → Fathom → Outlook → Teams → HubSpot; a source that can't be read is skipped and said), with a Summary (recipe `item.summary`) where one is missing or stale. With "with email": verify mode over all the account's open things instead, reusing the emails already read. Its one **Checked** line goes in the reply.

**Last, when the call was a meeting in CodyNote:** `codynote_refresh_queue_done(meeting_id, summary)` with that meeting's id and one line of what you suggested: a wrap-up the owner queued for it (Queue for Claude) is now done, so the hourly pass won't do it again. `nothing_queued` is fine: nothing was waiting.
